# FIN-POL-405: Corporate Travel, Meals & Business Expense Policy

## 1. Flight Bookings and Class of Travel
All commercial domestic flights under 6 hours flight time must be booked in Standard Economy Class via Navan (formerly TripActions). International transoceanic flights exceeding 8 continuous flight hours qualify for Premium Economy or Business Class with prior Vice President written authorization.

## 2. Daily Meal Allowances (Per Diem)
Employees on approved business travel are allocated a daily meal cap of $85 per day ($20 breakfast, $25 lunch, $40 dinner). Alcoholic beverages are eligible for reimbursement only during client entertainment dinners or sanctioned company celebratory events, limited to two standard drinks per person.

## 3. Expense Filing Windows and Receipt Mandates
Expense claims must be submitted in Concur within 30 days of the transaction date. Original itemized receipts showing VAT/tax breakdown are mandatory for any expense exceeding $25. Credit card summary slips without itemization are rejected by Finance Accounts Payable.
