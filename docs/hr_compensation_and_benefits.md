# HR-CONF-302: Executive & Staff Compensation Framework and Equity Bands

## 1. Salary Band Hierarchy and Annual Merit Cycles
Base salary bands are re-evaluated in November annually against 75th percentile tech market benchmarks. Annual merit reviews take place in Q1, with typical merit increase ranges between 3.5% and 8.0% based on Performance Rating (1 through 5). Out-of-cycle salary adjustments require People Committee and CFO signature.

## 2. Restricted Stock Units (RSU) Vesting Schedule
Equity grants follow a standard 4-year vesting schedule with a 1-year cliff (25% vesting after 12 months), followed by quarterly linear vesting of 6.25% thereafter over the remaining 36 months. For VP levels and above, double-trigger acceleration applies in the event of an approved change-of-control transaction.

## 3. Executive Retention and Bonus Pools
Annual discretionary performance bonuses for Director level and above target 25% to 40% of base salary, contingent on corporate EBITDA achievement benchmarks. Retention bonuses exceeding $50,000 carry a 24-month clawback provision if departure occurs voluntarily before the completion threshold.
