# STRAT-BOARD-001: 2026-2028 Strategic Corporate Roadmap & M&A Initiatives

## 1. Projected M&A Acquisitions and Core Expansion
The Board of Directors has authorized an initial capital allocation of $45M earmarked for strategic bolt-on acquisitions in the European cloud security compliance and AI observability space during Q2-Q3 2026. Non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) are currently executed with target code-names Project Boreas and Project Helios.

## 2. Market Geographic Expansion: APAC Region
Targeted market entry into Tokyo and Singapore is slated for Q4 2026 with a planned headcount expansion of 35 sales and engineering heads. Initial datacenter colocation footprint will leverage AWS ap-southeast-1 (Singapore) and ap-northeast-1 (Tokyo) regions.

## 3. Revenue Guidance and Capital Expenditure Targets
Projected Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) targets are set at $120M by end of FY2026, targeting gross margins of 78% and operating margin expansion to 22%. Capex budgets include $8M dedicated to sovereign GPU cluster reservation and enterprise infrastructure upgrades.
