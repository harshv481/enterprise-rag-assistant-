# HR-POL-101: Global Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## 1. Eligibility and Core Working Hours
All full-time and part-time staff who have successfully passed their initial 90-day probationary period are eligible for hybrid remote work, permitting up to 3 days of work from home per business week. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in the employee's local time zone, during which team members must be reachable via Slack and email.

## 2. Home Office Equipment Stipend
The company provides a one-time home office reimbursement stipend of up to $750 for ergonomic chairs, external monitors, keyboards, and desks. Claims must be submitted via Concur Expense within 60 calendar days of employment or formal transition to hybrid status, accompanied by original itemized receipts.

## 3. Internet Connectivity and High-Speed Requirements
Employees operating remotely are required to maintain a secure, high-speed broadband connection with a minimum baseline bandwidth of 50 Mbps download and 15 Mbps upload. Monthly internet subsidies of $50 are credited directly through the bi-weekly payroll cycle without requiring receipt submission.

## 4. International Remote Work Restrictions
Working remotely outside the employee's country of primary employment is restricted to a maximum of 30 calendar days per calendar year. Any international remote stint requires prior written approval from both Department Head and People Operations at least 14 business days before travel to ensure international tax compliance and export control adherence.
